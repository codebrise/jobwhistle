# Jobwhistle — Content Strategy & Distribution

---

## 1. Editorial Voice & Guidelines

- **Concise & Direct:** Avoid meaningless enterprise buzzwords ("disruptive", "paradigm shift", "revolutionary").
- **Engineer-to-Engineer:** Use accurate technical language (status codes, roundtrip latencies, SSRF mitigation, SHA-256 hashes).
- **Zero Fabricated Content:** Never invent customer logos, testimonials, or uptime statistics.
- **Calm Reassurance:** Focus on clear answers and quiet recovery during outages.

---

## 2. Organic Launch Channels

1. **GitHub Repository:** Open-source integration snippets and self-hosting documentation.
2. **Product Hunt & Hacker News:** Technical "Show HN" explaining the serverless edge architecture on Cloudflare Workers + D1.
3. **Indie Hackers & Dev.to:** Educational deep-dive on "Why your backup cron jobs fail silently and how to monitor them with heartbeats".
